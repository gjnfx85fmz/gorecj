What home remedies are safe for treating diarrhea in a 1-year-old?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

If your 1-year-old has diarrhea, safe home remedies can help ease symptoms and prevent dehydration. Two effective options are:

Cooked green bananas: Rich in resistant starch, they help firm stools and restore gut balance.

Homemade oral rehydration solution (ORS): Mix 1 liter of clean water with 6 teaspoons of sugar and ½ teaspoon of salt to replace lost fluids and electrolytes.

Offer small, frequent sips of ORS and continue breastfeeding or formula feeding as usual. Avoid sugary drinks, juice, and soda, which can worsen diarrhea.
