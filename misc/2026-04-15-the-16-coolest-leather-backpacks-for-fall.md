One of the quickest ways to upgrade your luggage: Start carrying a leather backpack. Pair its usability with its style, and you’ve got the perfect bag to take with you anywhere.

Pick a pack with the same discerning eye as you would a briefcase: Consider when you’ll be using it—on your commute, or for day trips or weekend adventures?— and which features you’ll need. From water resistance to adjustable straps to laptop compartments and exterior pockets, there’s a backpack out there for everyone.
