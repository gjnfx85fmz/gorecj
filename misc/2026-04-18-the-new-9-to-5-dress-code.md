# The New 9-to-5 Dress Code

In an era of disruption, your on-the-job clothes should feel just as cutting-edge as the ideas you serve up at the weekly meeting. And that calls for reimagining office essentials. The future-fit work wardrobe is one built on previously forbidden pieces like denim, bomber jackets, and trainers.

To ensure a smooth transition to this new state of sartorial affairs, we’ve tapped stylist Natasha Barraqué for tips on giving 4 casually cool essentials the corner office treatment.

The New Work Pants

Denim on-duty is 100 percent possible as more companies embrace a ‘startup’ mindset. The key to scoring HR’s approval? A tailored jacket and dress shirt — and the perfect fit. “Denim that fits well may be the most important element,” says Barraqué. “A medium to dark wash will pair best with almost any blazer color or print.” Chelsea boots or loafers and a rolled cuff amplify the look’s edgy-yet-elegant vibe.

The New Briefcase

Backpacks are quickly eclipsing the briefcase as the carryall of choice for burgeoning bosses everywhere. “City life may have a lot do with this shift. There seems to be a common understanding about carrying your entire life in your bag,” explains Barraqué. Amp up your accessories game with a leather or polished waterproof option, sticking to classic shapes and colors to keep things feeling profesh.

A killer commute calls for kicks that are equal parts practical and boardroom-ready. Skip the expected in favor of sleek sneakers with edge. “I love a leather sneaker — they always look polished and show onlookers you are bold and fashion-forward,” Barraqué says. Slip on a pair of dress socks to further elevate the look, then team with everything from streamlined suits to classic jeans.
