# How to Perfectly Tailor a Suit For an Athletic Build

At 6’6’’ and 220 pounds, Olympic swimmer Nathan Adrian isn’t exactly what those in the fashion world would call “sample size.”

“Most athletes notoriously have that V-shaped torso,” says Kevin Flammia, founder and CEO of RFM clothing, a menswear brand created specifically for athletes. “This means their shoulder sizes are going to be a lot larger than their slim waists.”

An athletic build can make it difficult for active guys to find clothes that fit properly. While Adrian is almost always photographed in swimwear, the Rio Gold Medalist routinely proves that, when it comes to dry-land style, he’s anything but a fish out of water.

The reason? Smart tailoring.

When Adrian puts on a suit jacket, it’s clear it was altered to fit his body.

If your sport coats and suit jackets typically end up looking either too big in the waist or too tight on the shoulders, here are three tips to help you nail fit like Adrian.

1. The Style

Guys with athletic builds should opt for a more casual, relaxed-fit sport coat, like this one from RFM ($348, rfmclothing.com). They aren’t constructed with multiple layers like more formal jackets, which can make it hard for a tailor to cut seamlessly.

2. The Shoulders

“If the shoulders don’t fit right, the rest of the jacket is thrown off,” says Flammia.

Before you make your purchase—certainly before you go to a tailor—make sure that the seam between the shoulder and arm hits the natural line where your shoulder meets your arm.

3. The Length

If you’re deciding between two different jacket lengths, opt for the longer one. That’s because it’s easier to cut fabric than to add to it.

The sleeve should hit the crook of your wrist, and the tail should hit midway down your butt.
