Understanding ADHD and Narcolepsy Basics

Before we dive into the 3-minute quiz, here's a quick snapshot of each condition:

ADHD (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder)

Core Features: Inattention, impulsivity, hyperactivity.

Trouble staying focused on tasks or conversations.

Frequently losing items (keys, documents).

Feeling restless or "on the go" even in calm settings.

Impact: Affects work performance, relationships, and self-esteem if unrecognized.

Narcolepsy

Core Features: Excessive daytime sleepiness, sudden muscle weakness (cataplexy), disturbed nighttime sleep.

Falling asleep unintentionally during routine activities.

Brief episodes of muscle limpness triggered by strong emotions.

Vivid hallucinations as you drift off or wake up.

When to Seek Immediate Help

Some signs demand prompt medical attention:

Chest Pain or Shortness of Breath: Could indicate heart or lung issues, not just tiredness.

Sudden Onset Confusion or Slurred Speech: May be a stroke in progress.

Loss of Consciousness: Even brief fainting warrants an urgent evaluation.

Severe Depression or Suicidal Thoughts: Contact emergency services or a crisis hotline right away.

For anything life-threatening or serious, call emergency services or visit your nearest emergency department. For non-urgent but concerning symptoms, always speak to a doctor.
