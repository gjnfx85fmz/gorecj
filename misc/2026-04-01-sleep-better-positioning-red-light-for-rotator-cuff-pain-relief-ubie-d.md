Positioning Red Light for Rotator Cuff Relief

Proper device placement and timing are crucial. Follow these guidelines:

Choose a device emitting 630–850 nm red or near-infrared light.

Ensure adequate irradiance (ideally 20–50 mW/cm²).

Clean the shoulder area; remove clothing or deodorant.

Sit or stand so the device can be stable 2–6 inches from your skin.

Treat each shoulder area for 5–10 minutes per session.

Aim for at least 3–5 sessions per week.

Consistency is key—schedule RLT at the same time daily (e.g., 30–60 minutes before bedtime).

If you experience unusual redness or discomfort, reduce time or increase distance.

Safety and Precautions

Red light therapy is generally safe, but keep these in mind:

Avoid looking directly into high-intensity LEDs; wear protective eyewear if recommended.

Do not place the device over open wounds or directly on metal implants without medical approval.

People with photosensitivity disorders or on light-sensitizing medications should consult a physician first.

Always follow the manufacturer's instructions for device use and maintenance.
