# Shave Your Face With This Awesome Star Wars Themed Razor

You might have already started growing out your Movember mustache—and for that, we salute you—but that’s no excuse to throw the rest of your grooming routine out the window.

Especially when you could be sabering the rest of your face with one of these new special-edition Star Wars razor packs from Gillette. (Just to be clear, the razors still use blades, not mini laser swords.)
