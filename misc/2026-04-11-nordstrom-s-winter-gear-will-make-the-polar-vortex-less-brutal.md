# Nordstrom’s Winter Gear Will Make the Polar Vortex Less Brutal

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

Harsh wintry conditions (like a polar vortex) are no friend to the man who wishes to live in fit-flops year-round. For a lot of men, the concept of winter dressing is one giant question mark. It’s incredibly difficult to put together a warm winter outfit that’s doesn’t make you look like a giant, round snowball. So, what’s a guy to do? That’s where Nordstrom comes in with the launch of their Concept 001: Out Cold. It’s the first in a series of curated physical and digital pop-up shops, part of Nordstrom’s New Concepts platform for customers to discover the latest product offerings from the most compelling brands.

The collection features 20 top-quality brands from around the globe that specialize in ski, snow, rain, and technical gear. Essentially, it’s a one-stop-shop for men to purchase all the gear they could need to brave the elements, without having to forgo style.

“Style and utility are equally important,” explains Sam Lobban, Nordstrom’s VP, Men’s Fashion. “My hope is that the Out Cold product offering demonstrates that you don’t have to choose one over the other. I think everyone should have a really good waterproof coat for winter, one with a three-layer membrane fabric to help keep you comfortable and dry.”
