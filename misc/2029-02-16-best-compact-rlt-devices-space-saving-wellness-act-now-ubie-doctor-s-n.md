Safety and Precautions

Always follow the manufacturer's instructions.

Wear protective eyewear if recommended.

Keep at least 6 inches between skin and panel unless your device specs say otherwise.

Don't stare directly into LEDs—even at low power.

Stop use and consult a healthcare professional if you notice skin irritation, unexpected rashes or worsening of symptoms.

When to Speak to a Doctor

If you experience any of the following, seek medical attention promptly:

Severe or worsening pain that prevents daily activities

Sudden swelling, redness or heat in joints

New, unexplained rashes or skin lesions

Any sign of infection (fever, chills, oozing wounds)

RLT can support wellness, but it's not a substitute for professional care. Always speak to a doctor about anything that could be life-threatening or serious.
