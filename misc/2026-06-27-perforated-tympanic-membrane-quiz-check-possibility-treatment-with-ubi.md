What is Perforated Tympanic Membrane?

A hole in the eardrum. It is typically caused by direct injury, but can also be caused by severe infections in the middle ear (otitis media).

Typical Symptoms of Perforated Tympanic Membrane

Difficulty hearing in one or both ears

Earache

Ear pain worsens when pulling on the ear

History of injury to face

Feeling of blocked ears

Bleeding from the ears

Significant difference in hearing difficulty between my left and right ear

Ear discharge

Diagnostic Questions for Perforated Tympanic Membrane

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are you experiencing difficulty hearing in one or both ears?

Do you have an earache?

Does pulling on your ear make the pain worse?

Have you ever been struck on the cheek or hit hard on the face?

Do you feel like your ears are blocked?

Treatment of Perforated Tympanic Membrane

Most cases heal without treatment. Surgery may be considered to patch the hole if it does not heal or repeatedly gets infected or drains.
