Ever since the pandemic hit and the country went into quarantine, we've been forced to adapt to a less-than-ideal set of circumstances, to say the least. Instead of commuting to work, we're setting up our laptops on the coffee table. Drinks with friends have been switched out for Zoom happy hours. And following the closure of gyms, fitness enthusiasts had to come up with some creative workout solutions that they could manage at home, with limited space or equipment.
