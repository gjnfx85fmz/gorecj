What is NAD+?

Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide (NAD+) is a coenzyme present in every living cell, playing a pivotal role in converting nutrients into cellular energy. It is essential for energy production, cellular repair, and overall health. As we age, NAD+ levels naturally decline, leading to reduced energy, cognitive decline, and increased susceptibility to age-related diseases. Restoring NAD+ levels may help counteract these effects, supporting overall health and longevity.
