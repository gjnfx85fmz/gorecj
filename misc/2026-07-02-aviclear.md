How Does AviClear Differ From Other Acne Treatments?

To understand how effective AviClear™ works, it’s important to understand how acne starts. Acne forms when sebum – the oily substance on your skin – combines with dead skin cells and clogs your pores. AviClear™ treatment differs from other acne treatments because it targets the oil-producing sebaceous gland bringing it back to pre-pubescent levels, making a big difference in your results. AviClear™ resolves acne at the source by selectively targeting the sebocytes and suppressing sebum production.

Most other acne treatments target the cause of acne but not sebum production. Topical and oral therapies can offer temporary results with severe side effects.
