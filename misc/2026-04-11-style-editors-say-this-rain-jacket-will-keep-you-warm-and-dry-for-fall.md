What To Consider

Waterproof

As you search for the best men's rain jackets, you should be able to distinguish between those that are waterproof versus options deemed water-resistant. Waterproof jackets are designed to completely repel water and keep you dry in the midst of heavy downpour or prolonged exposure to moisture. In contrast, water-resistant items shed water to a certain degree, providing protection against light rain or splashes. The latter may eventually allow water to penetrate under stronger conditions.

Materials

Check the quality of construction to ensure the rain jacket is comfortable, durable and long-lasting. Materials such as Gore-Tex, nylon and polyester excel at repelling water, but their effectiveness at blocking moisture from jackets can also restrict airflow and trap heat, resulting in a lack of breathability. To address this issue, some jackets incorporate ventilated panels to enhance relief as you wear them for extended periods. Brands like Arc’teryx and Columbia are highly regarded for strategic placement of such features throughout their garments.

Packability

Consider whether you need a rain jacket that is lightweight and packable for easy storage and portability. Look for options that can be folded or rolled for convenient carrying in a backpack or suitcase. Some styles feature innovative designs that allow them to be balled up and packed away into one of its pockets, perfect for minimalists and for those who like to travel light.

Features

Look for reinforced seams or seam-taped zippers, which work to keep water out of your pockets. Another essential feature is zippered ventilation panels that are usually positioned at the back or underneath the armpit to offer an excellent sense of airflow. Some styles include reflective accents to catch the attention of passing cars, ensuring nighttime visibility while walking—a crucial aspect, especially in New York City.

Difference Between a Daily Rain Jacket and a Hiking Rain Jacket

Every guy has—or should have—a daily rain jacket they turn to when the weather is nasty outside. And depending on where you live some of us need a daily rain jacket more than others. The easiest way to distinguish these types of jackets is by examining the overall durability of the jacket and testing how easy it is to throw on with your casual outfits. A daily rain jacket is also typically sturdier, thicker, and more durable because it's been designed with versatile environments in mind. It's true you can wear a daily rain jacket for hiking, but keep in mind this is a garment made for general purpose use. A good one way to think about the two types of rain jackets is by comparing them to what it would be like wearing a walking shoe while hiking versus a hiking boot.

A hiking jacket is typically going to be lighter and more packable. They're also usually more expensive, as the materials used are special blends of fabric made to bat away moisture and not weigh you down. It's entirely possible you could wear a hiking jacket for daily wear, but if you care about comfort then a daily rain jacket will appeal more to your liking. Hiking jackets are made for a specific use, and their tech fabrics can sometimes be too over-the-top for something as simple as wearing them to work or walking your dog.
