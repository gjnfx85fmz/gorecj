# 9 Exercises to Replace Crunches

Yesterday was the 35th anniversary of Bruce Lee's mysterious death. Lee, whose abdominals may be more famous than all other six-packs, including Brad Pitt's in Snatch, has left us with many things, including this quote:

"Absorb what is useful, Discard what is not, Add what is uniquely your own." - Bruce Lee

And when it comes to abdominal workouts, I urge you to discard crunches as the main exercise in your abs programs.

Frankly, if you want to have a flat stomach or 6-pack abs, you never have to do another crunch or sit-up ever again. Sure, they might be tough. Even a real challenge...

But there is no need to ever lie on a sweaty gym mat used by who-knows-who how many times without cleaning, or worse, a hotel room carpet if you are doing crunches while traveling...
