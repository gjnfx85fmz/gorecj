About this lesson

Enhance your expertise in treating gummy smiles and achieving balanced, natural lip aesthetics for your patients. This advanced training video offers a detailed, step-by-step approach for medical professionals, covering key concepts such as lip and muscle assessment, targeted product application, and the precise techniques needed to improve smile appearance. Learn how to address concerns like bruxism, thin lips, and excessive gum exposure through expert-led instruction.The video provides a comprehensive, evidence-based strategy to correct gummy smiles by relaxing specific facial muscles, increasing lip resistance, and ensuring proportional harmony between the upper and lower lips. With a focus on delivering natural, subtle results, you'll gain the confidence to perform effective treatments that enhance facial aesthetics and improve patient satisfaction, all while maintaining a refined, professional approach.
