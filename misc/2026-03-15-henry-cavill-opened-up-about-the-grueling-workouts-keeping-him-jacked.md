# Henry Cavill Opened Up About the Grueling Workouts Keeping Him Jacked for 'The Witcher'

[Cavill Opened](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/)

During a recent interview (remote, in lockdown) with Variety’s Marc Malkin, Man of Steel and Justice League star Henry Cavill spoke about his training regimen for his latest role, Netflix’s The Witcher.

“For the physical aspect of the character, I was working with Dave Ramsey, my trainer, for both the aesthetic and what was required of me for stunts,” Cavill said. “It was a very grueling training regimen when you add it to a TV schedule.”

Cavill plays the sword-wielding Geralt of Rivia, a Witcher—a.k.a. mutant monster hunter—which required hours of continuous shooting, meticulous choreography, and difficult stunt work. With such a blistering schedule, Cavill frankly often didn’t have time to learn every bit of choreography.

[Jacked for The Witcher'](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/3552273490.html)

“I’ve done some swords before… but because I was shooting so much, there was no opportunity for me to learn these fights,” he said. “They would send me a video [of the choreography] and I would try and learn the fight through these videos, and then ultimately have to learn it either the morning of, during the rehearsals, or the night before.”

Injuries, well, ensued. A little.

“Injuries happen,” he said. “You do catch each other. In a perfect world, you never touch skin. But with all the rehearsals, you end up making contact. The trick is pulling that contact before it does any real damage.”

While no one really knows when season 2 will start shooting because of lockdown, Cavill’s had to keep himself at some level of decent shape. He could get the call any day now.

“After the initial ‘I’m going to start drinking from noon’ thing and eating whatever I can lay my hands on, I started getting myself back into shape,” he says. “I’m going to make sure that I’m in a certain place before we restart shooting. The pressure’s off to change my body, and it’s more about maintenance.”

Cavill even chatted a little about the Snyder Cut of the Justice League, which will come to the HBO Max steaming service in 2021.

“With everything that’s happened this year, with the lockdown and cinemas having to close and streaming services now being how we’re getting our entertainment, it’s not really a surprise,” he said. “I think it’s one of those things where it’s an opportunity, and I think it’s great that Zack finally has an opportunity to release his vision. I think that’s really important for a storyteller and a filmmaker.”
