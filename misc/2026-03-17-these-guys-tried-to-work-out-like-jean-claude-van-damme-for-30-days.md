# These Guys Tried to Work Out Like Jean-Claude Van Damme for 30 Days

[Tried to Work Out](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/4986828971.html)

[Tried to](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/blog/7830974509.html)

For their most recent fitness challenge, vlogging brothers Rob and Arno commit to an entire month of training like their childhood hero, the "muscles from Brussels" himself, action movie legend Jean-Claude Van Damme.

The actor, known for his roles in Kickboxer, Bloodsport and Street Fighter (not to mention those Coors Light and Volvo Trucks commercials) is still shredded at 60 years old, maintaining his physique—not to mention that famous flexibility—with a strength and mobility routine which he shared earlier this year.

[Van Damme for](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/blog/0442319564.html)

[Van Damme for](https://peplawllc.com/)

[for Days](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/blog/7879111836.html)

For 30 days, Rob and Arno threw themselves into a combination of bodyweight exercises, kettlebell workouts, lifting weights, and running, carrying out all of the physically intense workouts at home due to the widespread closure of gyms during the pandemic. In addition to their training, they also made changes to their nutrition for the month, cutting out all junk food and alcohol from their diets for the duration of the challenge.

And it's not long before they're both struggling to keep up their motivation to continue in the face of the aches and fatigue that the workouts inflict on them.

[Tried to](https://weight-signal.sunsimiaosd.com/)

"It's crazy how bad shape I am in," says Rob. "Training every day has been interesting. But eating fries and other crap is something I've missed a lot."

Arno, meanwhile, is encouraged when he begins to see some physical results—although the workouts never get any easier. "I'm seeing some changes, I'm feeling better, but it's hard. It's harder than I thought," he say. "I've never been this sore in my life... I just ran 12 km. Never again in my life. It was awful. It felt like dying, but really slowly."

[Out Like](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/2970085366.html)

At the end of the 30 days, Rob has lost 4.3 kgs (almost 9.5 pounds), with a visible increase in muscle growth in his arms and in definition in his abs. Arno, meanwhile, has lost 6.4 kgs (14.1 pounds) and is noticeably leaner.

And while both brothers are extremely happy with the results they have achieved through hard work and a sensible diet, the first thing they do to celebrate the end of the 30 days is to eat everything they've been denying themselves. As Rob puts it: the occasional treat can contribute to a "positive mindset."
