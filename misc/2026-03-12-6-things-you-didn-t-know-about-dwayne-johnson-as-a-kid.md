You know Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson as one of the most recognizable action stars in the world. The guy has twenty movies in the works, not to mention a new reality fitness show and another season of Ballers on the way.

But how did The Rock get to be the legendary star he is today? Johnson himself may be the first person to tell you that it took an incredible, nay, a nearly unbelievable amount of work and perseverance to get to where he is today. Here are 6 things to know about Dwayne Johnson as a child.

Where was Dwayne Johnson born?

[Johnson as Kid](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Dwayne Johnson was born in Hayward, California, on May 2, 1972, to Ata Johnson and WWE star Rocky Johnson.

He started working out when he was 14

According to Johnson, he started hitting the gym at the tender age of 14. It wasn’t just to get jacked, but rather, it was to fight the hopelessness he felt after his family was evicted from their home.

"I reeeeaaaaalllyy hated that feeling of helplessness and never wanted that to happen again,” Johnson wrote on Facebook. “So, I did the only thing I could control with my own two hands in hopes that one day my family would never worry about being evicted again — I trained."

Johnson first hit the gridiron at 16

By the time Johnson turned 16, he looked like this:

Already hitting his peak height of 6'5" and weighing in at more than 200 pounds, Johnson was a force to be reckoned with on the field and off. Thanks to his size, he was recruited by his high school football coach Jody Cwik.

“He said, ‘I want you to do something for me... I want you to come out and play football for me,’” Dwayne explained to OWN. Of Cwik, Dwayne said, “I love that man. I’ll never forget the impact that he had on my life. My takeaway from that amazing relationship that I had was the empathy that he had for a punk kid who treated him so rudely and disrespectfully. He looked past that BS and said, ‘I believe in you and I want to turn you around.’”

Thanks to his newfound dedication to football, Dwayne explained, “My grades got better, and I started getting recruited from every college across the country.” He added, “My thought process started to change. That’s when I started thinking about goals and what I wanted to accomplish.”

He got a full ride to Miami on an athletic scholarship

At the age of 18, Johnson was awarded a full football scholarship to the University of Miami. He was the only freshman to make it onto the field with the team. Though that pressure, Johnson revealed, lead him to dabble in steroids.

"I tried them when I was 18, me and my football buddies. Nothing happened," he told The Hollywood Reporter. So he kicked the habit before it overcame him. He finished his college career with more than a few solid games under his belt, and a Bachelor of General Studies in Criminology and Physiology.

Dwayne worked through depression as a young man

As a freshman at U Miami, Johnson sustained a shoulder injury that sent him into a severe depression.
