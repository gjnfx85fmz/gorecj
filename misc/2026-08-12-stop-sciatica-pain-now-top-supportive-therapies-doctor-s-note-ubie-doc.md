Internal Medicine

Supportive therapies for sciatica include physical therapy, targeted stretching exercises, and heat or cold treatments. Physical therapy strengthens core and back muscles to reduce nerve pressure, while alternating heat and ice packs helps ease inflammation, muscle spasms, and pain. Together, these methods can improve mobility and support long-term recovery.

Because sciatica symptoms can overlap with other conditions—like a herniated disc, piriformis syndrome, or spinal stenosis—it's important to understand what's actually causing your pain before choosing a treatment path.
