# Tom Cruise Opens Up About That Gruesome 'Mission: Impossible' Ankle Injury

Tom Cruise shattered his ankle filming a stunt for Mission: Impossible 6

Speaking at CinemaCon, the actor revealed he only had a few weeks to get his broken ankle back in working order

While filming the movie, Cruise also performed a daring Halo jump from a military plane 106 times

Tom Cruise is shedding more light on the grisly ankle injury he sustained while filming a stunt for the upcoming Mission: Impossible 6.

[Gruesome Mission Impossible' Ankle](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/)

If you could handle the slow-motion video of the stunt, then you saw Cruise attempting to jump from one rooftop to another, coming just a few inches short, slamming into the wall, and shattering his ankle. Because he's Tom Cruise, he finished the stunt — then called cut and went to the hospital.

He couldn’t take time off from the film forever. As Cruise recently shared, he had just a few weeks to get his ankle in order, get back in shape, and get back to set.

[Tom Cruise Opens Up](https://peplawllc.com/blog/0505379639.html)

[Opens Up](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/)

“I went right into rehab. About 10 hours a day, 12 hours, seven days a week, because six weeks later I had to be on set, and twelve weeks later I had to be sprinting again,” Cruise, 55, said at CinemaCon on Wednesday, according to People. “Doctors said they weren’t sure whether I’d be sprinting in nine months, let alone that time. I was like, ‘Okay, I gotta figure this out and I gotta figure it out fast.’”

According to Cruise, he pushed through all the pain and was running sprints again six weeks later in rehab.

“I was running because all the sprinting in the film,” he shared with Extra. “I worked hard in rehab and training, and I gotta tell you it was not easy. It really hurt.”

While making an appearance on The Graham Norton Show back in January, the actor explained that even though his ankle was still technically broken, he had to get back to shooting because the film is still set to release on July 28.

Tom Cruise's Crazy Stunts

Sure, the ankle-breaking stunt seems dangerous, but it’s nothing compared to the High Altitude, Low Open (known as Halo) jumps he made for the film.

Cruise — who spoke about the stunt at CinemaCon — said he jumped out of a C-17 military plane an astonishing 106 times in order to get the scene just right, according to USA Today. He made the jump wearing a specially-designed suit and oxygen mask, because he'd be free-falling at speeds of 220 mph for an extended period of time.

“This movie is, without a doubt, the most epic Mission that we've made. It's a film that is adventurous, it's emotional and it's really cutting-edge,” Cruise said.
