# The Rock Uses 'Hobbs & Shaw' Teaser As a Chance to Roast Jason Statham's 'Tiny' Brain

The Rock and Jason Statham really want us to buy into their on-screen rivalry in the Hobbs & Shaw.

On Thursday, Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson shared a new teaser for the upcoming Fast & Furious spinoff on Instagram—and seized the opportunity to make fun of Statham's "tiny" brain.

In the caption of the video, The Rock wrote:

Zing.

Hobbs & Shaw follows the titular odd couple (played by Johnson and Statham, respectively) as they team up to take on Brixton (played by Idris Elba), a genetically-enhanced terrorist in possession of a dangerous biological threat. Of course, our heroes will also have to deal with their personal beef as they fight to save the day.
