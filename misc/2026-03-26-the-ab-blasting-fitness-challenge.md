# The Ab-Blasting Fitness Challenge

The plank is one of our favorite exercises at Men’s Health. That’s because it’s one of the best exercises for building core strength and endurance (read: chiseled abs). But most guys still skip it during their workouts. The reason: They think it’s too easy or boring. So Todd Durkin, C.S.C.S., author of The IMPACT! Body Plan, upped the intensity of the classic core exercise for this week’s fitness challenge. The 90-degree pushup hold gives you all the benefits of the original plank, but also blasts your shoulders, chest, and arms as if you were performing a pushup.

[The Ab-Blasting](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/)

Here’s how it works: Grab a stopwatch. Assume a pushup position with your body in a straight line from your head to your ankles. Slowly lower your body until your chest nearly touches the floor and your elbows are bent at 90 degrees. You should be hovering a couple inches off the ground. Hold this position as long as possible.Want to give it a try? Watch the video above to see Durkin demonstrate the 90-degree pushup hold. He held the exercise for two minutes and 7 seconds.
