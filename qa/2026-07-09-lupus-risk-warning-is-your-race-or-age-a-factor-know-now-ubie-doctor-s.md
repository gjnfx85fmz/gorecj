Lupus is a chronic autoimmune disease that disproportionately affects specific populations. Women of childbearing age (15–44) account for roughly 9 out of 10 lupus cases. It is also 2–3 times more common in Black, Hispanic, Asian, and Native American individuals compared to white populations, and often appears earlier and with greater severity in these groups.
