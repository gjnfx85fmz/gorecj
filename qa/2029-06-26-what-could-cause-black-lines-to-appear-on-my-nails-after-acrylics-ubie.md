Explanation

When you notice black lines on your nails after using acrylics, it could be due to damage or reactions caused by the chemicals in the acrylic products.

[could cause black lines](https://huanle3.com/dlvs2yy/)

Possible Causes:

Chemical Reaction: Acrylics contain chemicals that can cause changes in the nail, such as methyl methacrylate, which might lead to nail discoloration or damage.

Nail Damage: The process of applying and removing acrylics can weaken or damage the nail, leading to changes like black lines.

What to Do:

Observe: Keep an eye on the nails to see if the lines change or go away.

Consult a Professional: If the lines persist or worsen, consider consulting a healthcare provider for advice.

It's important to ensure that any nail products used are applied by a professional and that you follow all recommended aftercare steps to minimize potential problems.
