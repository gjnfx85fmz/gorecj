How to Rehydrate After Vomiting?

The best way to rehydrate after vomiting depends on the severity of your vomiting and how long it has been since you’ve thrown up last. If it has been less than six hours, you should be able to get the fluids back in by drinking water, soda, or sports drinks. What causes dehydration?

Dehydration occurs when you lose more fluids than you take in. Your body doesn’t have enough water and other fluids to perform its normal functions. Vomiting is one of the primary causes of dehydration. It can lead to rapid fluid loss, especially if you have diarrhea simultaneously. Other dehydration causes include sweating, exercise, fever, and hot weather.

Symptoms of dehydration

Dehydration occurs when your body doesn’t have enough water or fluids to function properly. When you vomit, your body loses fluids faster than it can take them in. The body loses fluids when you vomit. You can become dehydrated quickly if you don’t replace the fluids. Dehydration can cause dry mouth, tiredness, dark-colored urine, and dizziness. In severe cases, it can lead to more serious problems such as heat stroke or kidney failure.
