How much poop can your body hold at one time?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

The human body typically holds about 1 to 2 pounds of poop at any given time, though this amount can vary based on individual factors such as diet, hydration, body size, and bowel movement frequency. People who eat high-fiber diets or have less frequent bowel movements may retain more, while those with regular, healthy digestion often fall within the average range.
