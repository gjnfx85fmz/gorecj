What is the rapid PCR test?

A rapid PCR test is a nasal swab diagnostic that detects genetic material belonging to the SARS-CoV-2 virus to determine if you are actively infected with the Covid-19 virus. Unlike the RT-PCR test, this test can be processed onsite with results in 30 minutes or less.

What to expect from Rapid PCR accuracy

Rapid PCR tests, like their RT-PCR cousins, have an extremely high accuracy rate, with a Cochrane study finding 95% of samples correctly identified. Antigen tests, by contrast, have a one in five chance of returning incorrect results.
