White foam in urine might make you think about kidney problems. Kidneys help clean the blood and make urine. When there are kidney problems, like in Alport nephropathy, the kidneys do not work as well as they should. This can sometimes cause changes in urine.

Possible Causes:

Kidney Issues: Alport nephropathy is a type of kidney problem where the kidneys are damaged. This can affect how the kidneys filter the blood.

Other Health Conditions: Sometimes, other rare health conditions, like infantile sialic acid storage disease, can also affect the kidneys and change urine.

What to Do:

If you notice white foam in your urine, it is a good idea to talk to a healthcare provider. They can help find out what is going on and check if your kidneys are healthy. Remember, only a healthcare provider can give you the right advice for your health.
