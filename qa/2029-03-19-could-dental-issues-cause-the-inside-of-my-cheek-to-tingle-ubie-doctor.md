Explanation

The inside of your cheek is near many nerves and tissues that can be affected by dental issues. Some possible causes include:

Tooth infections or abscesses: Infections can irritate nearby nerves, causing tingling or numbness inside the cheek. Dental procedures: Treatments like fillings, extractions, or implants may temporarily irritate nerves, leading to tingling sensations. Nerve injury: Accidental injury to nerves during dental work or from trauma can cause tingling. Irritation from braces or dental appliances: These can sometimes press on soft tissues or nerves. Poor oral hygiene or gum disease: Inflammation can affect nerve endings in the mouth. If tingling is accompanied by pain, swelling, or persists for several days, contact your dentist or healthcare provider for evaluation and appropriate care.
