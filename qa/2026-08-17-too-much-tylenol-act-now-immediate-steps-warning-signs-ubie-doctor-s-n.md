What happens if I accidentally take too much Tylenol?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Taking too much Tylenol (acetaminophen) can be dangerous and may cause serious liver damage, liver failure, or even death. The maximum safe dose for most adults is 4,000 mg in 24 hours, but toxicity can occur at lower doses in some people. Early symptoms of an overdose include nausea, vomiting, stomach pain, and loss of appetite, though signs of liver damage may not appear for 24–72 hours.

If you suspect a Tylenol overdose, seek emergency medical care immediately—don't wait for symptoms to worsen. Because acetaminophen is found in many combination medications, accidental overdoses are common and easy to overlook.
