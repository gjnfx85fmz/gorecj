# Does Stress Make Narcolepsy Worse? Urgent Doctor’s Note Inside | Ubie Doctor's Note

Does stress make narcolepsy worse?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Stress does not cause narcolepsy, but it can significantly worsen its symptoms. High stress levels disrupt sleep quality, increase daytime sleepiness, and may trigger more frequent cataplexy episodes in people with narcolepsy type 1. Managing stress through relaxation techniques, consistent sleep schedules, and professional support can help reduce symptom severity and improve overall well-being.
