Having a swollen throat that makes it hard to swallow can be concerning. There are different reasons why this might happen.

Allergies: Some people experience throat tightness due to allergies. This can happen intermittently and may need medical evaluation to identify the cause and treatment options.

Injury: Sometimes, even a strong cough can lead to injuries like a laryngeal fracture, which could cause swelling and difficulty swallowing. This condition needs to be checked by a doctor to ensure proper care.

Medication Irritation: Certain medications, like doxycycline, can cause irritation in the esophagus, leading to swallowing difficulties. This is known as pill esophagitis and requires medical advice on how to manage the symptoms and possibly adjust medication.
