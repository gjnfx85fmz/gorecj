Explanation

There are several possible explanations other than a cold sore for a swollen bump on the lip. Here are some of the main causes to consider:

Lip abscess - A painful, pus-filled bump caused by bacterial infection that may develop after injury or irritation to the lip

Salivary duct cyst (mucocele) - A fluid-filled sac that forms when a salivary gland duct becomes blocked or injured

Allergic reaction - Sudden swelling that can appear as a bump, often accompanied by itching or tenderness

Fibroma - A benign growth made up of fibrous or connective tissue
