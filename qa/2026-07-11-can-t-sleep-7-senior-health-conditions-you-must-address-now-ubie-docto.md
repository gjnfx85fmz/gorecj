Explanation

As people get older, sleeping well can become more difficult. This can be due to several health conditions common in older adults.

[Must Address Now](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/blog/1669975473.html)

Common Sleep Problems:

Insomnia: Many older people have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep, which is called insomnia. This can be caused by stress, anxiety, or other health problems.

[Senior Health Conditions](https://peplawllc.com/blog/8207065779.html)

Sleep Apnea: This condition makes people stop breathing for short times while they sleep. It can cause loud snoring and make people feel tired during the day.

Other Health Conditions:

Chronic Pain: Conditions like arthritis can cause pain that makes it hard to sleep.

Heart and Lung Problems: Issues like heart failure or lung diseases can make breathing difficult at night.
