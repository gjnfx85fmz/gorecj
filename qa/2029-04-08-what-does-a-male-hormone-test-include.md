# What Does a Male Hormone Test Include?

01 Jan What Does a Male Hormone Test Include?

Male hormone testing measures the levels of male hormones in the blood. Monitoring hormones is essential because imbalances can be related to some diseases, such as prostate cancer or erectile dysfunction. In this article, we’ll cover what to expect from hormone tests and when you should get them done.

Understanding male hormone tests

Male and Female refers to sex as determined by your chromosomes. If you’re born with XY chromosomes, you’re considered male; if you’re born with XX chromosomes, then you’re female. Your gender identity doesn’t necessarily need to match your biological sex.

Male hormone tests measure the levels of hormones in the body. They most commonly check testosterone levels, but they may also measure other hormones depending on the age and health status of the patient. The results of these tests can be used to detect and diagnose many conditions.

Your healthcare provider will likely request that you provide blood or urine samples for a male hormone test. Depending on your age and health history, your doctor may recommend additional testing. Hormone imbalances may require estradiol or prolactin tests to diagnose different conditions.

How does a testosterone test for males work?

Testosterone is an important hormone that plays many roles in men’s bodies.

Symptoms of testosterone imbalance can include:

Fatigue

Erectile dysfunction

Decreased sex drive

Difficulty concentrating

Sleep problems

Mood swings

Decreased muscle mass and strength

Decreased bone mineral density

An increased risk for type 2 diabetes mellitus and coronary artery disease

However, patients with normal total testosterone levels may still have symptoms due to elevated free testosterone levels.

A testosterone test measures the amount of testosterone in the body and looks for low, normal, or high levels of this hormone. The results may be used to determine any issues with the testes, pituitary gland, or hypothalamus gland.

Final Thoughts

Male hormones are essential for many body functions and also impact sexual health. You can get a blood or urine test to determine if you have abnormally high or low testosterone levels.

Following a test, you may be encouraged to make lifestyle changes, such as eating better and exercising regularly to help restore normal testosterone levels. Doctors may also prescribe medications to help alleviate symptoms and help you feel your best.
