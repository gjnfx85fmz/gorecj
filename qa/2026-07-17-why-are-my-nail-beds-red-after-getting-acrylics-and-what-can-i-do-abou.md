Explanation

Red nail beds after applying acrylics can be caused by an allergic reaction to the chemicals in the acrylic products.

[nail beds](https://peplawllc.com/)

What You Can Do:

Remove the Acrylics: If you suspect an allergic reaction, it is best to remove the acrylic nails to prevent further irritation.

Consult a Dermatologist: If redness or any other symptoms persist, a dermatologist can help determine the cause and suggest appropriate treatment.

Patch Test: Before using acrylics again, consider doing a patch test to check for allergic reactions.

Allergic reactions to nail products are not uncommon, and taking steps to identify and avoid the allergen can help prevent future issues.
