Both Tylenol (acetaminophen) and Advil (ibuprofen) are effective over-the-counter options for relieving sore throat pain. Tylenol works well for mild to moderate pain and is gentler on the stomach, while Advil reduces both pain and inflammation, making it especially helpful when swelling is a key symptom. The best choice depends on your specific symptoms, medical history, and any other medications you take.

Since a sore throat can stem from many causes—viral infections, strep, allergies, or acid reflux—identifying the root cause is essential for choosing the right treatment. Explanation

Acetaminophen© or Ibuprofen, which treat fever and pain, are often effective against sore throats as well. Both medications will not treat the underlying cause of the sore throat like a viral infection. As the medication wears off, the sore throat will return so repeated dosing is often necessary and safe.
