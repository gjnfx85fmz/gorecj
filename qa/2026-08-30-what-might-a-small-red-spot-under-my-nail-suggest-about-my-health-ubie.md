Explanation

A small red spot under your nail might be a sign of nail psoriasis. Nail psoriasis is a condition where the nails become affected, leading to changes like discoloration or spots.

What is Nail Psoriasis?

Nail Changes: It can cause your nails to change color, become thick, or develop spots.

Health Connection: Nail psoriasis is often linked to skin psoriasis, a condition affecting the skin.

What to Do?

If you notice changes in your nails, it's important to talk to a healthcare provider. They can help determine if it is nail psoriasis or something else. Remember, changes in your nails can sometimes be clues about your overall health.
