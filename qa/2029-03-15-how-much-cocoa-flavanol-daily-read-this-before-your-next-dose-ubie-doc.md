How much cocoa flavanol should I take daily?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Daily consumption of cocoa flavanols has been shown to improve blood flow and lower blood pressure, according to multiple studies. While the optimal dose varies by individual, research consistently links regular intake—typically 200–600 mg per day—to measurable cardiovascular benefits, including enhanced endothelial function and reduced hypertension risk.
