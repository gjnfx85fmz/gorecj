Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Apple cider vinegar (ACV) may help lower post-meal blood sugar levels, though effects and optimal dosing vary between individuals. Research suggests consuming 1–2 tablespoons of diluted ACV before meals may improve insulin sensitivity and reduce blood sugar spikes, particularly after carbohydrate-heavy meals.

However, blood sugar fluctuations can stem from many underlying causes—including prediabetes, hormonal imbalances, or other metabolic conditions—that ACV alone won't address.
